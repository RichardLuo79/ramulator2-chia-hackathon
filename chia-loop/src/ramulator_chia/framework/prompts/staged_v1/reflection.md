# End-of-round summary

The candidate, measurements, and promotion decision are frozen. Write
`notes/summary.md`.

Record what changed, its physical rationale, measured improvements and
regressions, diagnostic evidence, failed approaches, the reviewer's decision,
unresolved issues, and useful next experiments. Distinguish observations from
hypotheses.

Preserve knowledge even when the candidate was rejected. Do not modify the
model. The next round receives this summary and the campaign's permitted history.
